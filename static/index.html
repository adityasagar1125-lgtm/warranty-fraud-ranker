1. Why the 97% Accuracy Target is a Trap

The Board's Paradox: In an imbalanced dataset where fraud is ~1.2% (141 of 11,146 claims), a dummy model that approves every single claim achieves 98.8% accuracy while catching zero fraud and stopping ₹0 in losses!

2. Root Cause: The May 1, 2026 Policy Change

On 1 May 2026, claims under ₹2,000 were auto-approved without inspection to cut turnaround times. This created an immediate gaming incentive:

Pre-May 2026 Small Claims
0.5% fraud
Mandatory partner inspection for all claims
Post-May 2026 Small Claims
3.2% fraud (6.4x spike!)
35 of 36 post-May frauds are small claims < ₹2k
Key Insight (Meenal was right): The fraud is not "all new partners". Post-May small claims at genuine established partners have only 0.1% fraud (1/924). The fraud is heavily concentrated in a handful of rogue outlets (e.g., SP3160, SP3232, SP3318, SP3207).

3. The 3-Layer Architecture Solution

Layer 1

Gradient-Boosted Classifier

Calibrated HistGB + XGBoost ensemble on tabular ratios, customer history (0→0.8%, 1→1.8%, 2→5.4%, 3+→21%), days since purchase vs warranty, and text signals.

Layer 2

Entity-Risk & Empirical-Bayes Shrinkage

Shrinks outlet fraud rates toward cohort prior ($m=10$), preventing unfair penalties on clean new partners while isolating serial reuse and threshold bursts.

Layer 3

Capacity-Aware Markowitz Rupee Ranker

Ranks claims strictly by expected savings minus risk variance penalty, serving the exact 40 highest-value claims to the desk per month with transparent audit reasons.

4. Honest Failure Modes & Risk Mitigations

  • False Positive Goodwill Bill: Holding a genuine claim costs ₹380. Because our ranking maximizes net expected savings, each check in the top 40 has $p \ge 5\%$ for large claims and $p \ge 49\%$ for ₹400 claims, ensuring expected rupee savings are strictly positive.
  • Unseen Partners (14 in test set): Empirical-Bayes shrinkage automatically falls back to the partner type prior for unseen outlets, preventing false blacklisting.
  • Label Noise (Legacy Zoho 0s): Legacy Zoho zeros (which hid undecided outcomes) are down-weighted ($w=0.75$) during training to prevent noise contamination.
  • Adversarial Adaptation: Retrain monthly and monitor outlet burst velocities to detect new gaming thresholds.
